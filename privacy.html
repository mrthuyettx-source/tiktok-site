Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Starlink Group, including the website https://www.starlinkgroup.vn/ and the TikTok integration provided by Starlink Group.

Purpose of the TikTok integration

When a user connects their TikTok account to Starlink Group, Starlink Group may use the permissions granted by the user through TikTok to identify the connected TikTok account and publish user-approved video content to that user's TikTok account through TikTok Content Posting API.

Information we may use

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, Starlink Group may process:

  • Basic TikTok account information needed to identify the authorized TikTok account.
  • Authorization data such as access token, refresh token, open_id, token expiration time, and related technical identifiers.
  • Video publishing status, publish identifiers, upload status, and related error messages.
  • Technical logs needed to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the TikTok integration.

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic: used to identify the TikTok account authorized by the user.
  • video.publish: used to publish user-approved videos to the authorized TikTok account.

How we use TikTok data

Starlink Group uses TikTok data only for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating and identifying the TikTok account authorized by the user.
  • Submitting and publishing user-approved video content to TikTok.
  • Checking the status of video publishing requests.
  • Operating, maintaining, securing, and troubleshooting the TikTok integration.

Data sharing and sale

Starlink Group does not sell personal data. Starlink Group does not use TikTok data for unrelated purposes and does not share unnecessary personal data with third parties.

Token storage and security

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in the backend automation environment and are used only to perform actions authorized by the user. Starlink Group applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authorization data and system logs.

Revoking access and data deletion

Users may revoke Starlink Group's TikTok access at any time through their TikTok account settings or by contacting Starlink Group. After access is revoked or disconnected, Starlink Group will stop using the TikTok permissions for new publishing actions.

Users may request deletion of data related to the TikTok integration by contacting Starlink Group at mrthuyettx@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy questions about Starlink Group or the TikTok integration, contact: mrthuyettx@gmail.com.
