Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Starlink Group TikTok App.

Information we use

When a user authorizes Starlink Group TikTok App with TikTok Login Kit, the app may use basic TikTok account information such as open_id, display name, and avatar, depending on the permissions granted by the user.

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic: used to identify the authorized TikTok account.
  • video.upload: used to upload videos to the authorized TikTok account.
  • video.publish: used to publish videos directly to the authorized TikTok profile.

How we use data

TikTok authorization data is used only to operate the user's own video publishing workflow. The app does not sell user data and does not share unnecessary personal data with third parties.

Token storage

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in the backend automation environment and are used only to perform authorized TikTok publishing actions.

Data deletion

Users may revoke app access from their TikTok account settings. After access is revoked, the app will no longer publish content for that TikTok account.

Contact

For privacy questions about Starlink Group TikTok App, please contact Starlink Group through the official website.
