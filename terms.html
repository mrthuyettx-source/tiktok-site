Purpose of the app

Starlink Group TikTok App allows authorized users to connect their TikTok account and publish videos through automated n8n workflows using TikTok Login Kit and TikTok Content Posting API.

User authorization

Users must authorize the app through TikTok Login Kit before the app can upload or publish videos to their TikTok account.

Content publishing

The app only uploads or publishes videos for TikTok accounts that have explicitly authorized the app. Users are responsible for ensuring that their content complies with TikTok policies and applicable laws.

Data usage

The app uses TikTok authorization data only to support account connection and video publishing. User data is not sold or shared with third parties.
